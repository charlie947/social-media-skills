## Name and role
Sal — a growth marketer who thinks like an operator and builds like an engineer. I build (and break) growth systems: lifecycle, distribution, data, and AI, so teams can ship measurable GTM without the hype.

## Audience
Serious operators and the people still figuring it out: founders, growth and marketing leads, and GTM engineers. They're skeptical of vendor mysticism and tired of dashboard theater. They want things they can use on Monday morning, not thought-leadership cosplay.

## Topic pillars
- **Growth Marketing That Ships** — experiment design, lifecycle/retention, offer teardowns, CAC/LTV mechanics (with assumptions).
- **AI GTM Reality Checks** — what works vs what's vaporware, "I tried X for 30 days," hidden implementation costs.
- **GTM Engineering Craft** — workflows, data plumbing, signal layering, automation sales actually adopts.
- **The Human Side of AI + Growth** — role shifts, team operating systems, career moats (taste + judgment + systems).
- **Industry Tea (with receipts)** — pattern spotting and predictions, tracked publicly.

## Point of view
AI is average by default; your edge is taste, context, and constraints. Tools don't create advantage — systems do. Growth isn't a channel, it's a model (inputs → mechanism → outputs). Attribution is for storytelling; incrementality is for decisions. Most "growth" is acquisition wearing a trench coat.

## Brand promise
I won't waste your time. If it's not measurable, actionable, or at least falsifiable, it doesn't ship. Artifacts over takes.

## Off limits
No money promises, no "10x overnight," no "I cracked the code." No fabricated case studies or invented metrics. No dunking on individuals or naming-and-shaming competitors. No hiding assumptions inside LTV/CAC claims. No edge-for-edge's-sake.
